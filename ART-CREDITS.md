# Art credits

Current pixel artwork generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-27. Assets: `game/assets/garden-pixel.png` and `game/assets/flowers-pixel.png`. These replace the earlier storybook artwork in the game. The flower atlas uses CSS sprite positions and pixelated rendering without a soft mask. No image-editing scripts were used.

## Pixel garden prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original PIXEL ART scenery for Rare Friends Dream Garden game. Landscape 1536x1024. True low-resolution raster game art: design as a 192x128 pixel image enlarged by exactly 8x nearest neighbor, every visible pixel a crisp square, consistent coarse pixel grid, stair-step outlines. Top-down slightly isometric quiet garden clearing, no characters. Strict palette: ink black #111111, paper white #eeeeea, mid-gray #787873, pale-gray #b6b6b0; tiny signal-lime #ccff00 accents only. Strong 1-bit retro handheld game spirit with sparse ordered dither shadows. Border of chunky pixel trees, angular rocks, low white picket fence at upper edge, small square pixel pond at far left lower edge with geometric ripples. Crescent moon motif subtly in dark top border. Central 65 percent a broad empty LIGHT GRAY dirt/grass clearing for 6 interactive rectangular plots to be overlaid later. Empty center, a few sparse pixel grass tufts only. Thin simple stepping-stone path lower edge and left lower corner so a small caretaker can stand. No flowers or planting spots in center, no UI, no text or logo, no characters, no gradients, no soft lighting, no bloom, no anti-aliasing, no brush texture, no realistic details. Crisp restrained pixel craftsmanship and bold black/white contrast.

## Pixel flower atlas prompt

Create a flat retro videogame flower sprite atlas. EXACT 3 columns x 2 rows on landscape 1536x1024 canvas. Pure solid #111111 black background right up to the hard edges of all plants, uniform everywhere. Only flat #eeeeea white, #b6b6b0 gray and a few #ccff00 lime pixels. Draw at 192x128 virtual resolution then enlarge by 8x nearest neighbor. Every individual pixel block square with crisp hard edges. No soft light, no glow, no halo, no gradient, no texture, no shadows. Must look like actual low-resolution 1-bit handheld videogame sprites NOT a painting of pixel art. Six whole isolated plants centered in their equal cells with generous margins. Top row: white daisy, two hanging bells, three stars. Bottom row: orchid, sunflower, lotus. Each has chunky square white leaves and straight stepped stems. Distinct six silhouettes. Plant height about 35 coarse pixels in 64-pixel-high cell. Black flat background, no text or borders.
